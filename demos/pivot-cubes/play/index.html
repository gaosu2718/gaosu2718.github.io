Actions

How it moves

  • A cube rolls over a corner it shares with a touching cube: one beside it, or one diagonally across that corner.
  • It can't swing through other cubes.
  • The swarm must hold together. Cubes hold by sharing an edge, or by touching at two corners (a pincer or a Λ); one corner alone doesn't hold.
  • A cube that would land hanging on one corner rolls on around it instead.

Controls

  • Drag a cube one cell: one step per drag
  • Hover a cube, then WASD or arrows: one step per press
  • Drag empty space or middle-drag to pan · scroll to zoom
  • Cube colour: on a target cell, not yet
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
  • R reset · N new · F fit · G ghost · T theme
